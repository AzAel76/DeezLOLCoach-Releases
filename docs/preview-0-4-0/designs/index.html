LoL Coach 0.4.0: three layout designs

Three different ways to organise the app: what's on screen when, and how you move around. Each uses the Fluent Dark theme; the themes (Fluent Dark, Hextech, Clean Light) apply to any of them. All three keep what the tester asked for: the window opens itself at champ select, settings sit in the menu at the top left, the crest icon, the full item set, and the in-game phase card unchanged. Made 2 October 2026 against v0.3.4, with example data.

1 · Command Center2 · Game Flow3 · CompanionCompare

1 · Command Center

Everything at once, on your second screen. No pages to click through: one wide screen with the draft on the left, the game plan in the middle and your loadout on the right. The strip at the top follows the game, and each column updates in place (in game, the middle column shows the current phase and the reminder timeline moves along).

2 · Game Flow

One step at a time, following the game. A step bar across the top moves on by itself, and each step shows only what matters then, in large type: in champ select, your loadout and a short "before you load in" checklist; on the loading screen, the confirmed lanes and the game plan; after the game, the review. History slides in from the right edge.

3 · Companion

A slim panel that docks beside the League client. For one screen: the panel snaps to the client's edge and stays out of its way. Sections are cards that open and close, and the one that matters now opens by itself. It can expand to a full window on a second screen.

My recommendation: Game Flow, with the Companion's compact mode as an option later. It fits the "clean and simple" feedback best, works on any screen setup, and its checklist is a natural home for the focus from the last review. Command Center is the strongest for second-screen players; it could become a "show everything" view inside Game Flow.

Questions for the tester: which layout feels right in a real game day: 1, 2 or 3? And do you play with a second screen?
