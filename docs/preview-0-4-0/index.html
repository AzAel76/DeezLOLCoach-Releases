Design draft · not the current app

LoL Coach 0.4.0 mockups

Proposed look for the next major release, based on tester feedback: a native-feeling Windows app with one main window (settings menu at the top left, everything else inside, happy on a second screen), cleaner see-through overlays, and a new coach icon. All screens use example data.

These are mockups for feedback, made 1 October 2026 against v0.3.4. Nothing here is final, and some details (such as exact layouts and colours) will change while building it.

1. App icon: three coach concepts

Each keeps the gold and deep blue of the current icon so it's still recognisable, and has to read at 16 px in the tray. A is a coach's whistle, B a tactics clipboard with a play drawn on it, C a shield-shaped crest with a whistle, closest to League's own crest style.

My pick is A: it says "coach" at a glance and survives being shrunk to the tray. C looks most like League but loses its whistle below 32 px.

2. Main window: Home

One window replaces the tray menu. The menu at the top left holds the quick actions; the left pane holds the pages. It's a normal window, so it can sit on a second screen, and closing it keeps the app running in the tray. Home answers "is everything ready?" at a glance: client connected, AI working, what's next.

3. Main window: Champ select

Everything for the next game on one page, filling in as champ select goes: both teams with their confirmed or assumed lanes, the rune page and summoner spells (already set in the client), and the full item set: six items plus boots, which is also saved to the client so it shows in the in-game shop. Small overlays in game; the detail lives here on your other screen.

4. In game: cleaner overlays

Fewer, quieter overlays: a frosted, see-through phase card with a thin progress bar to the next phase (laning ends at 14:00), a compact CS tracker, and reminders as a slim banner instead of a box. Each can be moved and resized, and all of them fade further when you press Tab for the scoreboard. Still clock-only: nothing reacts to what happens in the match.

6. Settings

Opened from the menu at the top left, in the style of Windows 11 Settings: categories on the left, one row per setting, each with a plain description. Voice gets its own page with several voices to preview.

7. Questions for the tester

  1. Icon: A (whistle), B (clipboard) or C (crest)?
  2. Main window: should it open on its own when champ select starts, or only show a notification?
  3. Item set: is one full build (six items plus boots) enough, or do you want a second, situational set as well?
  4. Overlays: is the phase card the right size at 1080p? Would you rather it sits top-right (as now) or next to the minimap?
  5. Voice: which matters more: a deeper "coach" voice, or a faster, shorter delivery?
